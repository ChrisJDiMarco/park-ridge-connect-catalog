Park Ridge Connect Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Park Ridge Connect is a resident guide for Park Ridge, New Jersey. The app is designed to work without accounts, advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking.

Information We Collect

The app does not collect personal information, does not require a login, and does not operate a user-data backend.

Public Data Sources

Park Ridge Connect fetches public information such as borough updates, National Weather Service forecasts, and a place directory generated from OpenStreetMap. These requests are used to show current local information in the app.

Speech Features

Voice input is optional. If you choose to use speech recognition, the app uses Apple platform speech and audio services to turn spoken questions into text. You can also use typed questions instead.

Third-Party Services

The submitted no-key build does not include advertising, analytics, or a third-party AI account service. If a future build enables an external voice or AI provider, this policy and the App Store privacy answers should be updated before release.

Data Sharing and Sale

Park Ridge Connect does not sell personal data and does not share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Contact

For privacy questions or app support, use the public support issue tracker.
